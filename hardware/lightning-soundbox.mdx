Payment soundboxes have transformed digital payments for small merchants across India — a speaker on the counter announces each incoming payment out loud, so shopkeepers can confirm payments without stopping to check a phone. The Lightning Payment Soundbox brings that same experience to **Bitcoin Lightning payments**, as fully open hardware anyone can build, repair, and improve.

## What It Does

When a customer pays a Lightning invoice, the soundbox announces the confirmed payment amount out loud and shows it on a built-in display — giving merchants instant, hands-free confirmation at the point of sale.

- **Audible payment confirmations** — spoken amount announcements through an onboard speaker
- **Multilingual audio** — confirmations in English, Hindi, and Kannada, with more languages planned
- **Visual confirmation** — a colour TFT display shows payment status and amount
- **Low-cost, open design** — targeting an affordable bill of materials (~\$17) so it's viable for small merchants

## Hardware at a Glance

| Component | Role |
| --- | --- |
| ESP32 | Main microcontroller — Wi-Fi connectivity and application logic |
| ST7735 TFT display | Colour display for payment status and amounts |
| MAX98357A I2S DAC | Digital audio output driving the speaker |
| Speaker | Voice announcements of confirmed payments |

## Software Stack

- **Firmware** for the ESP32 handling display, audio playback, and payment notifications
- **GGWave audio transmission** for over-the-air data transfer to the device
- **BTCPay Server plugin** (C# / .NET 8) connecting the soundbox to a merchant's BTCPay Server instance so payment events reach the device

## Why Open Hardware for Payments?

Commercial payment soundboxes are closed devices tied to a single payment provider. When the provider changes terms, ends support, or shuts down, the hardware becomes e-waste. An open soundbox means:

- Merchants own their hardware outright — no rental fees or provider lock-in
- Anyone can repair a failed unit with off-the-shelf parts
- The community can add languages, features, and integrations
- The design can be manufactured locally, anywhere in the world
