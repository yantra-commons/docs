Yantra Commons is a nonprofit based in Bengaluru, India, dedicated to making hardware as open and collaborative as open-source software. Every project we publish includes complete schematics, PCB layouts, a bill of materials, firmware source code, 3D-printable enclosures, and step-by-step build guides — all under open licenses you can use freely.

## What you'll find here

Yantra Commons publishes two categories of hardware:

- **Open alternatives** — fully open recreations of popular closed-source or proprietary devices that are often designed with expensive spare parts or intentional obsolescence in mind.
- **Original designs** — new hardware we've created from scratch, shared openly so the community can build, modify, and improve them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the project library">
    Visit [Browsing Projects](/hardware/browsing-projects) to find a device that interests you. Each project page links to its full repository.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Source your components">
    Use the project's bill of materials to order parts. Our [Sourcing Parts](/hardware/sourcing-parts) guide walks you through finding affordable components.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and flash">
    Follow the [Assembly](/hardware/assembly) and [Firmware](/hardware/flashing-firmware) guides to put your device together and bring it to life.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share and contribute back">
    Improve a design, fix a bug, or publish your own project. See [Contributing](/contributing/overview) to get started.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  All Yantra Commons designs are released under open licenses. You can build them for personal use, modify them, and even use them commercially — check the [Licensing](/concepts/licensing) page for details on each project's license.
</Tip>
