Yantra Commons is a nonprofit, and our work — publishing complete, open hardware designs anyone can build and repair — is made possible by the organisations that support us. Their fellowships, manufacturing support, grants, and tools let us keep every design free and open. We're grateful to each of them.

## Become a Sponsor

Yantra Commons welcomes support from manufacturers, foundations, and companies who share our belief that hardware should be open, repairable, and owned by the people who use it. Sponsorship can take many forms — manufacturing and in-kind support, grants, fellowships, or tooling.

If you'd like to support open hardware, reach out through the [Yantra Commons GitHub organisation](https://github.com/yantra-commons) or connect with us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/yantra-commons).
